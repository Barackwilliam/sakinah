Safety Tips

  • Complete your profile fully and honestly
  • Verify your identity and photos
  • Keep personal information private
  • Communicate only within the platform
  • Report suspicious or inappropriate behaviour
  • Meet in public places if you decide to meet
  • Trust your instincts

What We Do Not Allow

  • Fake profiles or impersonation
  • Inappropriate photos or content
  • Harassment, abuse or hate speech
  • Requests for money or financial help
  • Sharing personal contact details early
  • Any illegal activities
